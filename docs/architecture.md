\# BunnyEra AI Architecture



BunnyEra AI is the intelligence layer of the BunnyEra ecosystem.



\## Core Role



It stores and organizes:



\- Agent definitions

\- Prompt templates

\- Workflow definitions

\- Model configuration

\- AI usage examples



\## Relationship With Other Repositories



\- bunnyera-console: UI control center

\- bunnyera-ai: AI brain and configuration

\- bunnyera-claw: execution engine

\- bunnyera-security: risk and security rules

\- BunnyEra2FA: authentication layer



\## Execution Flow



1\. Console creates a task.

2\. AI selects prompt, agent, and workflow.

3\. Claw executes the workflow.

4\. Results return to Console.

5\. Reports are saved or sent to users.
